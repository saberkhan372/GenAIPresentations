# Sources, services, and reuse notice

## Historical corpus

The local **Founding & Rights corpus, 1776–1791** is derived from public U.S. National Archives transcriptions:

1. [Virginia Declaration of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/virginia-declaration-of-rights)
2. [Declaration of Independence](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript)
3. [U.S. Constitution](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript)
4. [Bill of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript)

The corpus excludes editorial apparatus, headings, signatures, attestations, the two unratified 1789 proposals, and Amendments XI–XXVII. Its tokenizer normalizes case and punctuation, splits hyphenated forms at the hyphen, and never matches across a source-block boundary. See `development-notes.md` for the instructional reason for that boundary.

## External services

The presentation can request content from services that are not part of this repository:

- [Long Story Short](https://sheets.works/long-story-short) is embedded on Slide 2 and also linked as a fallback.
- Google Fonts may provide the optional Figtree and Fraunces web fonts. System font fallbacks remain available.
- Participants may use an image-generation service during Part Three. No specific service is required or integrated into this repository.

Those services have their own terms, privacy policies, request logs, account histories, and availability. The presentation does not control or erase external-service data.

## Constructed examples

The prompt-and-output examples used in the labeling activity are constructed instructional illustrations. They are not represented as captured output from a named model.
